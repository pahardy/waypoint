# Instructor eval metrics

- **Pass** (headline): every applicable check passes. Answer cases: all key
  facts stated, grounded, valid citations. Decline and not-covered cases:
  expected behaviour, grounded, valid citations. Off-topic: the no-model path.
- **Key facts**: share of a case's key facts the judge finds stated correctly.
- **Grounded**: no rule, number or procedure stated without passage support,
  and no FAA material presented as Canadian (judge).
- **Behaviour**: the instructor did what the case kind requires (judge).
- **Valid cites**: every bracketed label matches a passage it was given (code).
- **Expected cite**: at least one expected section cited (code; diagnostic).

Judge: claude-opus-5-5 at effort high. Results are directional: no held-out split.
